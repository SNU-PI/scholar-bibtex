Deep learning builds on a handful of papers.
Residual networks~\cite{he2016deep} and the Transformer~\cite{vaswani2017attention}
changed model design, BERT~\cite{devlin2019bert} popularized pre-training,
Adam~\cite{kingma2015adam} became the default optimizer, and
GANs~\cite{goodfellow2014generative} introduced adversarial training.
